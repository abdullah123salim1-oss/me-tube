    How it works

    1

    Copy the link

    On Instagram, tap ⋯ → Link (or “Share → Copy link”) on any public Reel, video, photo or carousel.

    2

    Paste & fetch

    Drop the link above. We resolve the post and show a real preview with every quality option we can find.

    3

    Save in HD

    Hit download — the file streams through our proxy, so it saves straight to your device in original quality.

    FAQ

    Which posts are supported?

    Public Reels, videos, photos and carousels (/reel/, /reels/, /p/, /tv/, and /share/ links). Private accounts and stories need a login and are not supported.

    Is the quality original?

    Yes — we link the file straight from Instagram’s CDN and never re-encode it. When multiple qualities exist, you can pick one before downloading.

    Why does a link sometimes fail?

    Instagram blocks datacenter IPs and changes its page structure often. Running this app on your own machine (npm start → localhost:3000) is the most reliable setup.

    Is this legal?

    Download only content you own or have permission to save. Respect creators’ copyrights and Instagram’s Terms of Use — this tool is for personal use.
